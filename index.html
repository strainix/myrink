    Editing

    • Drag players and the puck. Drop the puck on a player to give him possession.
    • Passes fly straight to where the receiver ends the frame. The passer releases just in time.
    • A loose puck in front of the net is drawn as a shot. Select a player and press Backwards to mark his next move.
    • Bank a pass: select the puck and press Bank, or double-tap the pass arrow. Bank points snap to the boards.
    • Who's on the ice: ⋯ → Players on ice, or select a player and press Delete. Use it for power plays and penalty kills.
    • Bend a move: select a player and press Waypoint, or double-tap on his arrow. Drag the white handle to shape the curve; double-tap it to remove it.
    • Each frame shows where everyone stands and, with arrows, what they do next. The last frame has no arrows.
    • Every change shows up in the script, and script edits apply back to the board.

    Keys

    • Space play or pause
    • , . previous / next frame
    • ←↑→↓ nudge the selected token one cell
    • W add a waypoint to the selected player's next move
    • B toggle backwards skating for the selected player's next move
    • S open or close the script · Esc close panels
